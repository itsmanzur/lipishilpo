English (en-US) Hunspell dictionary
Source: LibreOffice dictionaries, derived from SCOWL (Kevin Atkinson)
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/en

Shipped as gzip (en-US.aff.gz, en-US.dic.gz) to keep the plugin small.
The editor decompresses them in the browser before Hunspell/nspell loads.
